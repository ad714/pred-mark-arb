Which edges survive the spread?

Football favourites priced 0.65 to 0.85 beat the vig-free closing line by 3.82% across 55,376 matches. That edge breaks even at 2.9 cents of round-trip cost. This counts how many Polymarket fixtures currently sit in that band and quote inside 2.9 cents. Cricket failed the same test at 9 cents.
